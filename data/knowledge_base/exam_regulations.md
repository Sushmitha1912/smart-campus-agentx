# Examination Regulations

1. End-semester examinations are conducted for a maximum of 100 marks per theory course, with a passing threshold of 40%.
2. Internal assessment (mid-term tests, assignments, quizzes) contributes 30% of the total course marks; the end-semester exam contributes the remaining 70%.
3. A student must have at least 75% attendance (or approved condonation) to be issued a hall ticket.
4. Makeup examinations are granted only for students who miss the end-semester exam due to medical emergencies, family emergencies, or approved official duty, supported by valid documentary proof.
5. Requests for makeup examinations must be submitted in writing to the Controller of Examinations within 5 working days of the missed exam, along with supporting documents.
6. Revaluation requests can be submitted within 10 days of result declaration, with a fee of ₹300 per course.
7. Malpractice during examinations results in cancellation of that course's exam and may lead to disciplinary action by the Academic Council.
