# Scholarship Criteria

1. The Merit Scholarship requires a CGPA of 8.5 or above with no active backlogs in any semester.
2. The Need-Based Financial Aid requires submission of an income certificate showing family income below ₹3,00,000 per year, verified by the Dean of Student Affairs.
3. The Sports Excellence Scholarship requires proof of a state or national level sporting achievement within the current or previous academic year.
4. Scholarship applications are accepted twice a year, at the start of each semester, through the Student Financial Aid portal.
5. Students receiving any scholarship must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance and no failing grades to continue receiving disbursements.

# Grievance Redressal Policy

1. Academic grievances (grading disputes, attendance disputes) should first be raised with the concerned faculty member, then escalated to the Head of Department if unresolved within 7 days.
2. Non-academic grievances (hostel, harassment, ragging, infrastructure) can be submitted anonymously or by name through the Student Grievance Portal.
3. The Grievance Redressal Committee is required to acknowledge a complaint within 48 hours and resolve or respond with a status update within 15 working days.
4. Grievances related to harassment or ragging are handled on priority by the Anti-Ragging and Internal Complaints Committee, with confidentiality maintained throughout.
