# Placement Policy

1. Students become eligible for campus placements starting from the 5th semester (3rd year), except for internship drives which may open from the 3rd semester for select companies.
2. Each company sets its own minimum CGPA, branch eligibility, and backlog criteria; these are published on the placement portal at the start of each drive.
3. A student who accepts and confirms an offer from a company is not permitted to sit for further placement drives in the same academic year unless it is a "dream company" as defined by the Placement Cell, in which case a "one dream company" exception applies.
4. Students must complete a mandatory resume review and a mock interview with the Placement Cell before being allowed to register for any core company drive.
5. Attendance at pre-placement talks and eligibility workshops is compulsory for students intending to sit for that company's drive.
6. Placement offers are subject to the company's own background verification and final round outcomes; the college does not guarantee employment.
