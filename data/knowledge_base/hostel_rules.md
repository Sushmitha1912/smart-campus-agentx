# Hostel Rules

1. Hostel gates close at 21:30 on weekdays and 22:30 on weekends; late entries require prior written permission from the warden.
2. Outstation leave requests must be submitted via the hostel portal at least 24 hours in advance, along with parent/guardian consent.
3. Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate expulsion from the hostel and disciplinary action as per UGC anti-ragging regulations.
4. Room changes are permitted only at the start of a semester, subject to warden approval and availability.
5. Mess fees are charged quarterly; a rebate is provided for approved leave periods exceeding 5 consecutive days.
6. Visitors are allowed only in the common visiting area during designated hours (16:00-18:00) and must register at the security desk.
