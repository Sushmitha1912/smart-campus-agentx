# Attendance Policy

1. A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory in each course to be eligible to write the end-semester examination.
2. Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may be granted condonation on medical or other genuine grounds, subject to approval by the Head of Department, with a fine of ₹500 per course.
3. Students with attendance below 65% are detained and must repeat the course in a subsequent semester.
4. Attendance for medical leave is only condoned if a medical certificate is submitted within 7 days of resuming classes.
5. Attendance shortage appeals must be submitted to the Examination Cell at least 10 days before the start of end-semester examinations.
6. Students on approved official duty (sports, hackathons, technical events representing the college) will have those days marked as "on-duty" and counted as present, provided prior written approval was obtained from the HOD.
