# Library Rules

1. Each student may borrow a maximum of 4 books at a time for a loan period of 14 days.
2. Overdue books incur a fine of ₹2 per day per book.
3. Reference books, journals, and rare collections are for in-library reading only and cannot be borrowed.
4. Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for at twice the current market price of the book.
5. The e-book and journal portal is accessible using the student's institutional login credentials from both on-campus and off-campus networks.
6. Silence must be maintained in the reading halls at all times; group discussion rooms are available on the first floor and must be booked in advance.
